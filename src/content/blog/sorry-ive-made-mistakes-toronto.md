This isn't an easy post to write, but it's one I need to write.

For a while, I was dating someone in this community. Over time, their actions and the things they said online caused genuine harm to people here. I kept the relationship quiet, I hid it, and whenever it got brought up, I wasn't honest about it. That was completely wrong of me, and I'm not going to make excuses or pretend otherwise.

I know that staying quiet and keeping secrets looks like I was enabling it, even if that wasn't my intent. By not being upfront, I made things worse and made it way harder for people who were hurting to trust what was actually happening. I take full responsibility for that. To everyone affected by what they did, and anyone who deserved honesty from me instead of being kept in the dark, I'm genuinely sorry. You deserved better from me, and I should have handled this the right way.

I'm not saying any of this to make excuses or try to spin what happened. I messed up. I'm not going to pretend otherwise, and I definitely don't expect people to just brush it off because I apologized. All I can do now is be completely honest, take actual responsibility, and let my actions going forward prove that I’ve learned from this.

Since then, I’ve been taking the time to work on myself. I started going to therapy to actually unpack everything and face the stuff I avoided, instead of just pushing it aside and hoping it would blow over.

I genuinely care about this community, and I want it to be a space that actually feels welcoming, fun, and safe for everyone. Trust matters, and building that back starts with me being completely honest, even when it’s uncomfortable.
