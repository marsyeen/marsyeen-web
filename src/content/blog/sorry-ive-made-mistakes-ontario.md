This isn’t an easy post to write, but it’s one that has been weighing on me, and it’s something I need to put out there publicly.

In the past, I was in a relationship with someone in this community whose actions and online behavior caused genuine harm to people here. While that was going on, I chose to keep the relationship quiet. I actively hid it, avoided questions, and wasn’t honest when it was brought up. That was completely wrong of me, and I’m not going to make excuses or pretend otherwise. **To be completely clear, that relationship has ended, and I no longer associate with or support that person.** But that ending doesn't change my past choices or how poorly I handled things while it was happening.

I’ve had to take a hard, honest look at what my silence meant. Staying quiet and keeping secrets protected a narrative instead of protecting people, and it understandably looked like I was endorsing or enabling what was going on. By not being upfront, I made things significantly harder for people who were hurting, and I broke trust that people had placed in me. To everyone who was directly or indirectly affected by what that person did, and to anyone who deserved honesty from me instead of being misled: I am deeply sorry. You deserved straightforwardness and integrity, and I failed to provide either.

I’m not writing this post to spin the situation, ask for sympathy, or expect anyone to brush this off just because I posted an apology. An apology is the bare minimum, not a clean slate. I made serious mistakes, and I own them fully. Over the past several months, I’ve been taking deliberate time to reflect on why I chose to avoid conflict rather than do what was right, and I am actively working on my personal growth and accountability so I never put myself or others in that kind of situation again.

Moving forward, my priority is straightforward honesty and respecting the community’s boundaries. I completely understand if people need space, choose not to interact with me, or need to block me, and I will respect those boundaries both online and at any future events. I care about this community and want it to be a healthy, safe space, and I understand that rebuilding trust isn't something that happens through a single statement. It has to be proven consistently through actions over time.

Thank you,
Mars (Lynix)
